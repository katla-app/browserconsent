Katla handles cookie banners for you by answering them on your behalf, the way you choose. It can only do that once you've consented to it yourself, so here is exactly what you would be agreeing to.

What BrowserConsent does

When a website asks for your cookie consent, BrowserConsent answers for you the way you choose: Reject all or Accept all. Where the website's consent platform offers a way to record that choice directly, BrowserConsent uses it; otherwise it presses the banner's matching button. If a banner has no way to reject on its first screen, BrowserConsent leaves it for you. It recognises Katla and many other consent platforms, including OneTrust, Cookiebot, Usercentrics, Didomi, Sourcepoint, TrustArc, InMobi Choice and Google Funding Choices.

What that means for your privacy

Rejecting all cookies limits websites to the cookies they strictly need, though some may switch off features that depend on the others, such as embedded videos.

Accepting all cookies allows websites and their partners to store and read information on your device for analytics, personalisation and advertising. That can include tracking you across websites, building a profile of your interests, sharing data with advertising partners and, in some cases, transferring data outside the EU/EEA. Each website stays responsible for how it uses your data, and its own cookie and privacy policies apply.

What stays in your control

  • Until you activate BrowserConsent below, it doesn't run on any website at all.
  • Banners you've already answered are left alone. BrowserConsent also steps aside as soon as you click or type on a page, so the choice there is yours.
  • You can pause BrowserConsent, or switch it off for specific websites, at any time.
  • You can switch between Reject all and Accept all in Settings. Accept all asks for your consent to it first.
  • Tracker warnings tell you when a website uses a common tracking cookie or pixel before you've answered its banner, or after you've rejected. They're on to start with, and you can turn them off in Settings.
  • You can withdraw consent on any website from the toolbar button. BrowserConsent asks the site's consent platform to record a refusal, or deletes the site's cookies.
  • You can withdraw this permission at any time in Settings. Consents already given stay valid until you withdraw them on each site, which Settings helps you do.

What Katla collects

Nothing. BrowserConsent has no servers and no analytics. Your settings, your consent receipt, the log of banners answered for you and the choices made on each website stay in this browser.
