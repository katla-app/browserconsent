Katla handles cookie banners for you by giving consent on your behalf. It can only do that once you've consented to it yourself, so here is exactly what you would be agreeing to.

What AutoConsent does

When a website asks for your cookie consent, AutoConsent gives it for you by choosing Accept all. Where the website's consent platform offers a way to record that choice directly, AutoConsent uses it; otherwise it presses the banner's "Accept all" button. It recognises Katla and many other consent platforms, including OneTrust, Cookiebot, Usercentrics, Didomi, Sourcepoint, TrustArc, InMobi Choice and Google Funding Choices.

What that means for your privacy

Accepting all cookies allows websites and their partners to store and read information on your device for analytics, personalisation and advertising. That can include tracking you across websites, building a profile of your interests, sharing data with advertising partners and, in some cases, transferring data outside the EU/EEA. Each website stays responsible for how it uses your data, and its own cookie and privacy policies apply.

What stays in your control

  • Until you activate AutoConsent below, it doesn't run on any website at all.
  • Banners you've already answered are left alone. AutoConsent also steps aside as soon as you click or type on a page, so the choice there is yours.
  • You can pause AutoConsent, or switch it off for specific websites, at any time.
  • You can withdraw consent on any website from the toolbar button. AutoConsent asks the site's consent platform to record a refusal, or deletes the site's cookies.
  • You can withdraw this permission at any time in Settings. Consents already given stay valid until you withdraw them on each site, which Settings helps you do.

What Katla collects

Nothing. AutoConsent has no servers and no analytics. Your settings, your consent receipt and the log of banners answered for you stay in this browser.
