          Privacy and legal information

          Data

          AutoConsent does not collect, send or sell any data. It has no servers, no analytics and no remote code. Your consent receipt, settings, excluded sites and activity log are stored in this browser's extension storage and are removed when you uninstall the extension. It reads the web pages you visit only to find and answer cookie banners.

          How your consent is handled

          • You authorise AutoConsent in advance, by ticking separate, unticked statements after reading what it does. Until then, and whenever you pause or withdraw, it doesn't run on any website.
          • Where a website's consent platform has a JavaScript API, AutoConsent records your choice through it; otherwise it presses the banner's "Accept all" button.
          • The exact wording you agreed to, the time and a fingerprint of the text are kept as your receipt, which you can export.
          • If the wording changes in substance, AutoConsent pauses until you agree again.
          • Banners you've already answered are left alone, and AutoConsent steps aside once you click or type on a page.
          • You can pause it, exclude websites, withdraw consent per website and withdraw your authorisation, each in a couple of clicks.

          Please note

          AutoConsent answers cookie banners on your behalf. Websites stay responsible for asking for consent correctly and for how they use your data. Rules on whether consent can be given in advance through a tool like this differ between countries, so a website may ask you again or rely on its own records.
